# Simonton 6500 Windows

## What it is
Simonton 6500 Windows are positioned as offering a wood-look aesthetic with the advantages of vinyl, plus energy-efficient options and color/laminate/hardware customization.

- Homeowners wanting a wood appearance without wood maintenance
- Buyers prioritizing styling + performance positioning

- “Look of wood” positioning + vinyl benefits
- Energy efficiency options
- Design customization options
