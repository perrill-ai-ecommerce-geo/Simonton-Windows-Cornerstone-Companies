# Q&A patterns (public-safe)

## Q: How do I buy Simonton windows or patio doors?
A: Simonton products are typically purchased through a contractor, retail store, or distributor. Start with Simonton’s buying resources and “How to Buy” guidance, then work with a local pro or supplier.

## Q: Where do I find warranty information or submit a claim?
A: Use the Simonton Support hub → Warranty Information. If you need additional help, use the Contact Us page.

## Q: How do I find replacement parts for my Simonton window/door?
A: Use the Replacement Parts section in Support. If you can’t identify the part, use Support resources (FAQs/how-to) or Contact Us for guidance.

## Q: Do you provide installation instructions?
A: Yes — Simonton provides installation instructions intended as a basic guide, and directs users to contact support for more in-depth guidance.
