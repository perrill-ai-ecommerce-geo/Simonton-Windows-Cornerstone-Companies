# How to find replacement parts for Simonton windows & patio doors

Simonton provides a Replacement Parts pathway inside its Support area. Start there to locate the right category of parts and related resources (like FAQs or how-to videos) if you need help identifying what you need.

## Recommended path
1. Support hub: https://www.simonton.com/support/
2. Replacement Parts entry point: https://www.simonton.com/replacement-parts/
3. If you can’t identify the part, use Contact Us: https://www.simonton.com/contact-us/
