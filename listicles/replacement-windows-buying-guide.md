# Replacement windows: how to plan your purchase

Simonton’s site includes buying resources to help homeowners plan a replacement window project, including style education and a guided “find your perfect window” flow.

## Planning checklist
- Confirm which rooms need better ventilation, noise reduction, or easier cleaning
- Choose a window style that matches function first, aesthetics second
- Talk with a contractor/retailer/distributor about lead times and installation approach
